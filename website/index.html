Tangible Worlds

Physical arrangement as spatial prompting for generative 3D environments

Screen recording of the working system, Part 2.2. A hand moves a wallet on the table; the generated house follows. Left and right are cropped from one recording and placed side by side; nothing else is altered.Part 2.2 recording. Top and bottom are cropped from one screen capture; nothing else is altered.

Hands set spatial structure.

Words set semantics.

Ordinary objects on a table become persistent handles. An iPhone tracks where they are. Language decides what they are. A generative pipeline gives each meaning a 3D form, and moving the object moves its counterpart without generating it again.

The following chapters follow one object through that path, using the project's own recordings, tracker output, logs and generated meshes. Where the page reconstructs something in the browser, it says so.

01

Physical sensing

The table is observed in depth, not recognised in pictures. An object is anything that stands above a fitted plane.

  1. Observe

    An iPhone looks straight down at the table.

    Record3D streams colour and LiDAR depth from the phone to a MacBook. The colour image is only for the person operating the tracker. Detection runs on the depth grid, which stays at 192 × 256 even when the colour preview is high resolution.

    Overhead RGB, 12 Aug 2026 · portfolio 02-LID-003

  2. Measure

    Depth separates things from the surface they sit on.

    An early diagnostic shows every region standing above the fitted table plane, with its height in millimetres. Real props appear alongside false regions from the wall, the table edge and sensor noise, which is why the final tracker adds a region of interest and height and footprint limits.

    depth_probe.py, read-only diagnostic · portfolio 02-LID-005 · dissertation Fig. 5.2

  3. Calibrate

    Thirty empty frames define the table.

    With nothing on the table, the tracker fits one plane and measures the residual noise. The foreground threshold is derived from that noise and clipped to 6–20 mm. If the noise exceeds 2.5 mm the calibration is refused and the operator must select the table again.

    Values from the frozen study tracker; final acceptance run noise 0.679 mm, threshold 6.0 mm

  4. Segment

    What rises above the plane becomes a region.

    Pixels more than the threshold above the plane are cleaned with a 5 × 5 morphological open and close, then grouped into connected components. Regions taller than 150 mm or smaller than 3 cm² are discarded. This is the tracker's own mask, shown at its native resolution.

    Frozen tracker debug output, 192 × 256 · portfolio 02-DEB-061/062

  5. Identify

    Each region receives a number, not a name.

    The tracker publishes geometry and a persistent id. Its label field is always the generic word "object". The first prototype used YOLO to name objects; the detector chose the vocabulary, so it was removed. The frozen tracker imports no recognition model.

    { "id": <int>, "label": "object",

02

Hands and movement

A hand is the largest thing on the table while it works. The tracker has to ignore it without forgetting what it was holding.

Frozen tracker debug frames at native 192 × 256, saved automatically when a new candidate appeared · portfolio 02-DEB-021 to 026

  1. Trackingid 3 follows its region
  2. Body presenta region touching the frame edge is treated as body, not prop
  3. Latchedwithin 3 cm of the hand, id 3 holds its last clean measurement
  4. Releasedfive consecutive clear frames
  5. Committednew position published; one move, not a trajectory

Handling is treated as information rather than noise. While a hand is near a known prop, the prop is not re-measured or re-learned; it waits. When the hand leaves, the new position is committed as a single move. The movement log therefore records before and after, not the path between.

Lifting an object off the table and setting it down elsewhere is recorded as a disappearance followed by an appearance.

Rather than inventing a new identity, the tracker checks whether the merged footprint is consistent with the sum of two known ones, and keeps both ids alive through the merge. How does the system calculate this?

The prop photographs and starting positions come from the recorded session. The tracker reports each object's position on the table as normalised x and y, from 0 to 1 across each edge; they are not centimetres. The same mapping, recorded.

          
The reconstruction ports the production renderer’s deterministic mapping: procedural sky, one shadow-casting key light, six drifting mist layers, line rain and a two-pulse lightning flash. Given the same twelve fields it produces the same scene. In qualification, five commands rendered three times each differed in 461 of 5,120,000 pixels, all inside a caption band.

          
Language is the unreliable part. Six of 68 cases failed both qualification runs, usually by changing one requested field and leaving another at its default. In the log above, make it night issued straight after a thunderstorm kept its rain and lightning; issued on its own, it did not.

08

        
Research, outcomes and limitations

      

      
Research question

      
How might tangible interfaces that translate physical arrangements into generative 3D environments support new forms of spatial authorship and creative exploration?


      

        
What the prototype demonstrated

        
    
          
  1. 1Neutral props keep tracked, numbered identities through hands and merges, and carry meanings assigned in words.
    2. 
          
  2. 2Moving a prop sets the arrangement; typed or spoken language sets what it means and the atmosphere.
    3. 
          
  3. 3Each meaning is generated once and cached, so moving a prop reuses its 3D model rather than regenerating it.
    4. 
        


        

          
What was not established

          
No participant study was conducted. Nothing here shows that people find the mapping intuitive, feel greater ownership or explore more freely.

          
Shallow props segment unreliably. The first generation of a meaning is kept, with no reroll. Terrain stayed a prototype. Object generation runs on rented GPU compute.

          

            All documented outcomes and limitations
            

              

                
Demonstrated

                
    
                  
  • Semantically neutral props tracked by LiDAR depth as persistent numbered handles, through hand occlusion and two-object merges.
    • 
                  
  • Meaning assigned in typed or spoken language, kept attached to the handle as it moves; meshes cached by meaning and never regenerated by movement.
    • 
                  
  • A two-stage generation path whose intermediate image locates where some failures begin.
    • 
                  
  • Explicit scale grammar, confirmed voice input, and a deterministic twelve-field atmosphere mapping.
    • 
                

              

              

                
Not established

                
    
                  
  • No participant study was conducted. A typed-versus-spoken study was designed and instrumented but not run.
    • 
                  
  • Nothing here shows that people find the mapping intuitive, feel greater ownership or explore more freely.
    • 
                  
  • Generated quality across arbitrary meanings, and interstitial relations across arbitrary pairs, were not systematically evaluated.
    • 
                

              

              

                
Limitations

                
    
                  
  • Shallow props remain unreliably segmented by the 192 × 256 depth grid.
    • 
                  
  • Appearance is sticky: the first generation of a meaning is cached, with no reroll or history.
    • 
                  
  • Terrain remained a prototype; amplitude from measured height was unstable under handling.
    • 
                  
  • Object generation runs on rented GPU compute, so shaped prompts leave the laptop.
    •
